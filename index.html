Features

Everything Henry does
to look after your tabs

Two focused features, nothing bloated. Henry is small, fast, and always asks before doing anything drastic.

▶️

Instant video pause

The moment you switch away from a tab with a playing video, Henry appears on your new tab asking if you'd like to pause it. One click — done. Saves serious CPU and battery.

💤

Tab sleep

Henry periodically scans for tabs you haven't visited in a while and flags them. You choose which ones to sleep. Sleeping frees memory — the tab reloads the moment you visit it again.

👁

Always asks first

Henry never does anything without your say-so (unless you enable auto-pause). Every action shows a friendly card on your current tab — you're always in control.

⭐

Eco score & levels

Earn points for every eco action — pausing videos, sleeping tabs, and keeping a daily streak. Level up from Seedling all the way to Giant as you save more energy.

🛡

Exempt any tab

Music, dashboards, work tools — click any tab in Henry's list to mark it exempt. Henry will never flag or sleep it.

⚡

Lightweight by design

Henry runs as a Chrome service worker with zero external requests. No analytics, no servers, no subscriptions. Just a tiny guardian living in your browser.
